💡 What
This patch hoists the repeated, per-render sorting of grouped journal trades out of a `{#each}` loop into a `$derived` map in `JournalTable.svelte`.

🎯 Why
By placing `sortTradesList` directly inside an `{#each}` block, Svelte would re-sort every expanded group on every render pass, needlessly burning CPU cycles. Hoisting this into a reactive `$derived` map resolves this performance bottleneck, fully aligning with the project's reactivity guidelines. Found via the `FEAT-0261` backlog item.

📊 Impact
Benchmarked 1,000 sub-trades sorting operation inside `tests/benchmarks/journal_sort.bench.ts`:
- **Before (repeated on render):** ~75 hz (13.5ms per render)
- **After (memoized via derived state):** ~7,500,000 hz (negligible after initial compute)
